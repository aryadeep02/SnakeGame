## 📖 About The Game

A modern twist on the classic Snake Game! Control your snake, eat food, grow longer, and try not to crash. Built with pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript - no frameworks needed!

### ✨ Why This Game Rocks

- 🎨 **Beautiful Design** - Neon colors and smooth animations
- 📱 **Play Anywhere** - Works on phone, tablet, and computer
- 🎯 **Three Difficulty Levels** - Easy, Medium, and Hard
- 🏆 **Track Your Progress** - High score saves automatically
- ⏸️ **Pause Anytime** - Take a break when you need it
- 🕹️ **Easy Controls** - Arrow keys or swipe on mobile

---

## 🎮 How To Play

### Controls

**On Computer:**
- ⬆️ `Arrow Up` - Move Up
- ⬇️ `Arrow Down` - Move Down
- ⬅️ `Arrow Left` - Move Left
- ➡️ `Arrow Right` - Move Right
- `Space` or `Esc` - Pause/Resume

**On Mobile:**
- 👆 Swipe in any direction to move

### Rules

1. 🍎 Eat the red food to grow longer
2. 🐍 Don't hit the walls
3. 🚫 Don't bite yourself
4. 📈 Each food gives you 10 points
5. ⚡ Game speeds up as you score more
